Parking lot

All drills

A multi-level parking lot with motorbike, car and truck spots. A vehicle enters, gets a ticket and a spot; on exit it pays a fee that depends on the time parked and the vehicle type. Model the classes.

Tag each responsibility below on exactly one class.

Responsibility check · 0%

  • ⬜Chooses a free spot that fits a vehicle
  • ⬜Knows whether a spot is occupied and what size it is
  • ⬜Issues a ticket that records the entry time and spot
  • ⬜Computes the fee from duration and vehicle type
  • ⬜Coordinates entry and exit across allocator, tickets and fees

Later Change Storms add responsibilities (a tier limit, say). A design that isolates each reason to change will absorb them without edits.
