A DESIGN-TACTICS RPG

Blueprint
Clash

Befriend Patternlings and Systemlings. Learn C++ low-level design and system design well enough to ace the interview.

The Workshop

Write C++ by hand. Hidden behaviour tests judge it, then Change Storms test whether your design bends or breaks.

The Siege

Draw an architecture and watch it face real traffic and chaos in a simulator you can reason about.
