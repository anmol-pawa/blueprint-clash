Tic-tac-toe

You are writing the rules engine of a two-player board game, starting with tic-tac-toe. Two players take turns marking cells on a 3x3 board; the first to get three of their marks in a line wins.

What you know so far:

  • Players are X and O, and X moves first.
  • A move names a row and a column; rows and columns start at 0.
  • The game reports whose turn it is, how it ended (won, drawn or still going) and what is in each cell.

Your task: design and implement it behind the ttt::Game interface in api.h. The hidden tests call only that interface. Ask the interviewer questions first: the brief is deliberately incomplete, and the game you are asked to build may not stay a 3x3 game.

The clock starts when you begin. Interviewers like candidates who ask before they build.
