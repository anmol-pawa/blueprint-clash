A bounded buffer

All puzzles

A buffer holds at most one item. One producer pushes two items; two consumers pop one each. All state is under one mutex, with while-loops around every wait.

Share one condition variable between 'not full' and 'not empty' and use notify_one: find the schedule where a wake-up goes to the wrong kind of waiter and everyone sleeps. (Spurious wake-ups are switched off here so the stuck state is visible; real code must still use while.)

notify_one wakes whoever has waited longest, maybe another consumer who finds the buffer empty and sleeps again, while the producer who needed the signal sleeps on.
